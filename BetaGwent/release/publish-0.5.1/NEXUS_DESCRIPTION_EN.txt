[size=5][b]Gwent Beta Classic — 0.5.1[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]Short summary[/b][/size]

Recreates standalone Gwent Beta 0.9.24 inside The Witcher 3, with five factions, 479 collectible cards, editable decks, new rewards, kegs and opponents using 46 deck archetypes.

[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]

Gwent Beta Classic replaces vanilla Gwent in The Witcher 3 with the cards and rules of standalone Gwent Beta 0.9.24. Matches remain part of the Witcher's world: play against NPCs, complete quests, buy cards and build your own decks.

The aim is to bring back Beta-era Gwent: three rows, bronze, silver and gold cards, leaders, weather, Consume, Deathwish, adjacent-unit interactions and the original board style. The board, interface and effects use original Beta materials and are being refined against historical references.

The mod is in active development. The AI considers placement, synergies, weather and the results of candidate actions, but it can still make poor decisions. Full visual parity with the original Beta has not yet been achieved.

[b]IMPORTANT: back up your saves before installing the mod and before every update. Bugs can still occur. Use a separate copy of your save for your first matches with a new version.[/b]

[size=4][b]Installation instructions[/b][/size]

1. Close the game and back up your saves.
2. Download either the Russian or English 0.5.1 package. Install only one language version.
3. When updating, remove the previous Mods/modBetaGwent0924 folder.
4. Extract the archive's Mods folder into your The Witcher 3 installation directory. The resulting path should be The Witcher 3/Mods/modBetaGwent0924/content.
5. Load your save. Open the game's Gwent deck menu to select or edit a deck, or talk to an NPC who plays Gwent to start a match.

The modBetaGwentReduxCompat companion is not used in this version; remove it if previously installed. Remove other Gwent overhauls, including Redux/Redo Expansion, and rebuild Script Merger output without the removed mods. Collections and decks from other overhauls are not migrated.

[size=4][b]Main features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]479 collectible cards, including 21 leaders, across all five Beta factions: Monsters, Northern Realms, Scoia'tael, Skellige and Nilfgaard.
[*]Five starter decks that can be edited directly, plus eight custom deck slots.
[*]Card catalog with filters, search, descriptions, crafting and named quest sources.
[*]Ordinary opponents using a pool of 46 deck archetypes, with predefined decks and rewards for quest opponents.
[*]Mulligans, your own deck viewer, both graveyards, full card inspection and placement between units.
[*]Faction-specific board halves, score counters, crowns, leaders and Beta-style status icons.
[*]Beta artwork, sound effects and card voice lines, with weather, damage, boost and Consume effects.
[*]Animation pace settings and reduced effects.
[*]Mouse, keyboard and controller support. Passing requires holding the coin with the mouse, P on the keyboard or Y on an Xbox controller.
[*]Separate Russian and English packages with localized text and card voice lines.
[/list]

[size=4][b]New in 0.5.1[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Three saved AI difficulty levels: Easy, Normal and Hard. Hard is the default.
[*]A six-page tutorial with inspectable card examples. Detailed inspection shows related summoned and created cards.
[*]A Speed Up button: press once to accelerate playback, again to skip. Keyboard: Space/Enter; controller: A.
[*]Opponent calculation is deferred until player action playback finishes. Refined Scorch, Geralt: Igni and Epidemic effects.
[*]Opponent Ambush identities are concealed in timers, audio and battle history.
[*]Collection, scraps, unopened kegs and deck compositions have an additional facts-based backup. This helps recover those values after saving without the mod if a backup already exists. Recovered decks use default names; previously lost data cannot be recovered. Full restoration of all settings and reward ledgers is not claimed.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Interface and controls[/b][/size]

Starter decks can be renamed and reset to their default lists. The catalog includes ownership filters, sorting and collection counters; the deck list shows why a deck is invalid. The action menu shows the last ten events with score changes and a saved AI turn delay: 0 / 0.4 / 0.8 / 1.2 seconds.

During a match, hold LT for your graveyard, RT for your deck, or both triggers for the opponent's graveyard. Release to return to the current selection. In the editor, LT/RT still remove/add a copy. Large choice lists fit on one page in two or three rows with previews; moving past an edge continues the list. Move down to reach buttons below the cards.

Abilities allowing up to N cards can be finished early. In a choice window, Show Board, Tab or Y temporarily reveals the field; repeat to restore the choices. Before placement, RMB cancels the selected card; during an ability already in progress, it opens inspection without confirming a target.

[size=4][b]AI and decks[/b][/size]

The AI considers remaining tutor targets, engine value, weather, draw spies, Deathwish activation and the chance of winning with the whole remaining hand. Updates refine decisive-round sequencing, conceding an unfavourable first round and combinations involving Fringilla, Draig, Draug, Light Longships, Thunder and Ambush cards. Response estimates use public information and bounded search; decisions can still be imperfect.

Eight lists were revised, and four similar decks were replaced with more distinct Dagon, Radovid, Usurper and Filavandrel variants. The total remains 46 archetypes. Selecting individual roster candidates does not establish balance across all decks.

[size=4][b]How to get cards[/b][/size]

[b]Starter collection.[/b] Five starter decks are available when you begin using the mod, one for each faction. You can edit them to suit your playstyle.

[b]Ordinary NPCs.[/b] Each of your first four wins against a particular ordinary opponent awards one bronze or silver card: four wins, one card per win. Duplicate rewards are possible; copies above the collection limit become scraps. Further wins award 20 scraps, and a loss awards 5.

[b]Quest opponents.[/b] The first rewarded win grants a predefined gold card. If you already own the allowed copy, it becomes scraps. Repeat wins against these opponents award 50 scraps.

[b]Kegs.[/b] Card merchants sell kegs for 200 crowns. Merchant stock replenishes; this is intentional. Open purchased kegs through the mod's menu: reveal the first four cards, then choose your fifth card from three options.

[b]Crafting.[/b] You can craft a card in the catalog even if you do not own it yet. Costs depend on rarity: 30, 80, 200 or 800 scraps. The selected card shows its exact cost. Cards with quest sources can also drop from kegs or be crafted.

[b]Excess copies.[/b] The collection keeps up to three copies of a bronze card and one copy of a silver card, gold card or leader. Copies above those limits automatically become scraps. Cards needed for your collection cannot be manually milled. One copy of every card and leader is enough to complete the collection.

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]

The Witcher 3 on PC. The current build targets game version 5.01. Earlier versions have not been verified for 0.5.1.

REDkit, Wwise and build tools are not required to play.

Mods that change Gwent, its menus, card trading or rewards may conflict. Compatibility with other Gwent overhauls is not guaranteed. Do not install RU and EN together.

[size=4][b]Current status and plans[/b][/size]

The project remains in active development. Compilation, archive checks and automated scenarios do not replace real-save, controller and quest testing in the normal game. Next priorities are update and save reliability, complex interactions, AI comparisons across all 46 archetypes, performance and interface refinement. No future release dates are announced.

[size=4][b]Bug reports[/b][/size]

Please include:

[list]
[*]Your mod and game versions, package language, and whether you played in the normal game or REDkit.
[*]Other installed mods, especially those affecting Gwent or menus.
[*]Both leaders and deck archetypes, relevant card names, round number and score.
[*]The actions leading to the issue, what you expected, what happened and whether it can be reproduced.
[/list]

Attach a screenshot or short video. If logging is available, copy the log immediately after the issue: it is usually Documents/The Witcher 3/scriptslog.txt, though your Documents folder may be redirected. Starting the game again can replace it. For reproducible issues, attach a separate copy of a save from before the problem.

For AI issues, a full match log, your deck list and the point where the opponent chose a poor card, target, row or pass are especially useful. Check files for personal information before sharing them.
