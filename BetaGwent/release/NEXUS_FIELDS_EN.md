Description

**You must back up your saves before installing the mod and before every update. Bugs can still occur; use a separate test save for your first matches.**

Gwent Beta Classic recreates the cards and rules of standalone Gwent Beta
0.9.24 inside The Witcher 3. It replaces vanilla Gwent while keeping NPC
matches, quests, merchants and card collecting connected to the Witcher's world.
The current release includes playable cards, deck building and progression;
Original Beta board and deck-builder artwork is included at a compact resolution suitable for the installed game.

Installation instructions

Close the game. Back up your saves before installation and every update: bugs can still occur. Download either the RU or EN package.
Extract its Mods folder into your The Witcher 3 installation directory.
The resulting path should be Mods/modBetaGwent0924/content. When updating,
replace the previous mod folder; do not install both language variants together.
Open the game's Gwent deck menu to edit decks, or talk to an NPC to play.

Main features

- 479 collectible Beta cards, including 21 leaders, across all five factions.
- Deck builder with eight saved slots, renaming, filters and pre-match selection.
- Five starter decks and a full card catalog showing unowned cards and sources.
- Random decks for ordinary NPCs; fixed decks and gold rewards for quest players.
- One bronze/silver card for each of the first four wins against an ordinary NPC.
- Buyable 150-crown kegs, opened individually, with no excess card copies.
- Automatic rounds, mulligans, both graveyards and your own deck view.
- Original artwork, sound effects and battle voices; separate full RU/EN packages.
- Mouse, keyboard and controller support, with contextual hints and card inspection.
- Shared AI logic and 40 adapted archetype profiles.

Requirements

The Witcher 3 on PC. Current build target: game 5.0; earlier versions have not
been validated. No additional mods, REDkit or Wwise are required to play.
Other mods changing Gwent menus, card trading or rewards may conflict.
Use one language package and back up your save before installation.
