[size=5][b]0.4.2 — changes since 0.4.1[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Mulligans now show a full hand in two rows of five, with a detailed hover preview. Cards no longer reorder during exchanges; replacements stay in the same slot. Updated controller navigation.
[*]Improved AI evaluation of Clear Skies, First Light and cards that provide access to them. Units that clear their own row choose an appropriate weather-affected row.
[*]Vran Warrior considers consuming fodder and spawning Arachas Hatchlings while preserving other engines on the board.
[*]Nekker Warrior considers Nekker deathwish chains when choosing deck copies. Exact simulations retain the strategic target when immediate results tie.
[*]AI passes an already-won round against a player with no cards or available leader when resolving round-ending effects confirms victory.
[*]Viper Witcher can damage a friendly unit when no enemy is available; AI evaluation accounts for mandatory friendly damage.
[*]Golden Froth uses the original 36-frame Beta animation, fixing disconnected square texture fragments. Reduced effects keep the foam still.
[*]Fixed the gap in the scrolling Skellige Storm texture.
[*]Reworked persistence of the four ordinary-NPC card rewards to address resets after loading a save.
[*]Replaced Isengrim with ordinary Iorveth in the Scoia'tael starter. Iorveth is added to existing collections without removing Isengrim or overwriting customized starter decks.
[*]Added a PowerShell build script for 0.4.2 and additional checks for gameplay synergies, collection migration, mulligan order and weather textures.
[/list]

Merchant keg restocking remains intentional.

Back up your saves before updating. Compilation and automated checks have passed; native UI and reward persistence after an actual save/reload still require testing in the normal game.
