# Gwent Beta Classic 0.6.2

Build for manual testing. Long comparisons of the old and new AI run separately.

- Restored the previous Cursed Ships and Foltest deck-boost rosters. The stage 128 revisions remain in the configuration, disabled with an explanation.
- Updated Thaler and four other draw spies: the AI considers card advantage, a safe lead after the spy, and a large deficit in a round it can concede. The extra discount is disabled in round three; an opponent pass or empty deck provides no extra card-pressure credit.
- Added ability-chain hints, detailed action history and public current card statistics.
- Added smooth hand movement, button/focus transitions, distinct recovery/resurrection accents and a large two-dimensional leader scene.
- The deck editor shows restriction reasons and the cost of missing copies. Confirmed card additions/removals can be undone; faction filters are remembered while the menu remains open.
- Kegs now highlight rarity, mark new cards when ownership is known, and reveal the first four cards together. The fifth card still requires an explicit choice.
- Flashes, shake, leader scenes and chain hints have separate session settings. Updated labels and embedded fonts cover all 13 text languages.

Build checks do not replace testing Scaleform, presentation and AI behavior in the game. This version makes no claim that the new AI has won the comparative tests.
