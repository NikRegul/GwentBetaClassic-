[size=4][b]About the mod[/b][/size]

Gwent Beta Classic recreates the cards, rules, board and progression of standalone Gwent Beta 0.9.24 inside The Witcher 3.

It replaces vanilla Gwent but stays part of the Witcher world:
NPC matches, quests, merchants, rewards and card collecting all keep working through the original game.

Version 0.3.0 brings the battlefield much closer to the original Beta: Beta fonts, card frames, HUD, mulligan screen, leader intro, row weather and effect animations, plus a new AI that looks ahead before every move.

[size=4][b]Main features[/b][/size]

[b]Cards and collection[/b]
[list]
[*]479 collectible Beta cards, including 21 leaders
[*]All five factions
[*]Full card catalog, including cards you don't own yet and where to get them
[*]One bronze or silver card for each of the first four wins against an ordinary NPC
[*]Fixed decks and gold rewards for quest opponents
[*]150-crown card kegs (Baron's castle Quartermaster), opened one at a time, no unnecessary duplicates
[/list]

[b]Decks[/b]
[list]
[*]Beta-style deck builder with 8 saved deck slots
[*]Five starter decks
[*]Deck renaming, filters (tier, type, neutral cards, owned cards) and search
[*]Pre-match deck selection with HD leader covers
[/list]

[b]Matches[/b]
[list]
[*]Beta board, HUD, fonts and card frames
[*]Leader intro with voice lines before the match
[*]Mulligan screen
[*]Original Beta row weather, drawn under the cards
[*]Effects play on all targets at once, and an arrow shows which card is acting
[*]Spying and revealed-card tokens from the Beta
[*]View both graveyards and your own deck during a match
[*]Original artwork, sound effects and battle voice lines
[*]Mouse, keyboard and controller support
[*]Widescreen support (side panels on ultrawide monitors)
[/list]

[b]AI opponents[/b]
[list]
[*]40 Beta deck archetypes; NPCs draw a random archetype of their faction every match
[*]The opponent's deck archetype is shown instead of the NPC name
[*]The AI simulates its candidate plays before choosing, mulligans by card value, avoids weather rows, places units for synergies and saves its leader for when it matters
[/list]

[b]Languages[/b]
[list]
[*]Separate full English and Russian versions
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Close The Witcher 3.
[*]Back up your save files.
[*]Download either the EN or the RU package.
[*]Extract the included Mods folder into your The Witcher 3 installation directory.
[/list]

The final path should be:
[code]Mods/modBetaGwent0924/content[/code]

[list=1]
[*]Start the game.
[/list]

Open the normal Gwent deck menu to use the new deck builder.
Talk to a Gwent-playing NPC to start a match.

[size=4][b]Updating[/b][/size]

[b]Back up your saves before every update.[/b]

Delete the old [b]modBetaGwent0924[/b] folder and install the new one in its place. Do not mix files from different versions.

Do not install the Russian and English versions at the same time.

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]

[*]The Witcher 3 on PC

[/list]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]

Mods that change these systems may conflict with Gwent Beta Classic:
[list]
[*]Gwent menus
[*]Card merchants or card trading
[*]Gwent rewards
[*]Vanilla Gwent progression
[/list]

If something goes wrong, test without other Gwent-related mods before reporting a bug.

[size=4][b]Reporting bugs[/b][/size]

The most useful reports name the exact card, opponent, deck or AI move involved.
If a match freezes or behaves strangely, please attach [b]scriptslog.txt[/b] from your The Witcher 3 folder: the mod writes what happened during the match there.

[size=4][b]О моде[/b][/size]

Gwent Beta Classic переносит карты, правила, игровое поле и прогрессию самостоятельного Gwent Beta 0.9.24 в The Witcher 3.

Мод заменяет встроенный гвинт, но остаётся частью мира Ведьмака:
партии с NPC, квесты, торговцы, награды и сбор коллекции работают через оригинальную игру.

В версии 0.3.0 поле стало гораздо ближе к оригинальной бете: шрифты, рамки карт, интерфейс, экран замены карт, представление лидеров, погода в рядах и анимации эффектов как в бете. Кроме того, ИИ теперь просчитывает ходы наперёд.

[*]Переименование, фильтры (ранг, тип, нейтральные, карты в наличии) и поиск
[*]Выбор колоды перед матчем с HD-обложками лидеров

[b]Партии[/b]

[*]Оригинальная погода беты в рядах, под картами
[*]Эффекты срабатывают по всем целям сразу, стрелка показывает, какая карта действует

Не устанавливайте русскую и английскую версии одновременно.

[size=4][b]Совместимость[/b][/size]

С Gwent Beta Classic могут конфликтовать моды, которые меняют:
[list]
[*]меню гвинта
[*]торговцев картами или обмен карт
[*]награды за гвинт
[*]прогрессию оригинального гвинта
[/list]

Если что-то не так, проверьте мод без других модов на гвинт, прежде чем сообщать об ошибке.

[size=4][b]Сообщения об ошибках[/b][/size]

Полезнее всего отчёты, где названы конкретная карта, соперник, колода или ход ИИ.
Если партия зависла или повела себя странно, приложите [b]scriptslog.txt[/b] из папки The Witcher 3: мод записывает туда ход партии.
