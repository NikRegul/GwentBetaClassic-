# Gwent Beta Classic — EN

Recreates Gwent Beta 0.9.24 cards and rules inside The Witcher 3, replacing vanilla Gwent while keeping NPC matches, quests and collection progression.

**Back up your saves before installation and every update. Bugs can still occur. Use a separate test save for your first matches.**

479 collectible cards including 21 leaders, five factions, eight saved deck slots, deck builder and full catalog. Ordinary NPCs choose from 40 archetypes. Your first four wins against each award one bronze/silver card. Quest players award gold, or 300 crowns if already owned. The Baron's quartermaster sells 150-crown kegs: up to four bronze copies and one rare choice. Caps: bronze 3; silver/gold/leaders 1.

HD Beta presentation, automatic rounds, mulligans, both graveyards, own-deck viewer, sounds and voice lines. Mouse, keyboard and controller: D-pad/stick navigation, A confirm, B cancel, X inspect, LB/RB rows or pages. The game's swapped accept/cancel setting exchanges A/B. See CONTROLS_EN.md for full controls.

English text and voices. Install only RU or EN and remove an older manual installation of the same mod. Targets PC 5.0; other Gwent/menu/reward mods may conflict. Players do not need REDkit or Wwise.

0.2.4 fixes Bear creation, nested plays, row choices and cursor requests, and restores inline HD textures. Installed-game acceptance is pending. Next: archetype strategy tuning, better combo forecasts and animation/weather polish.

Reports: version/language, retail game or REDkit, card names and preceding actions, round/scores/hands, screenshot and log if available.
