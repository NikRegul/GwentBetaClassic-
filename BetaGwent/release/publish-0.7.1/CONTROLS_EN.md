# Controls 0.7.1

Mouse: left click to select/confirm, right click outside replay to inspect/cancel an uncommitted selection. During replay, hold RMB anywhere for temporary acceleration and release to restore tempo. Short clicks never accelerate or skip. Space/Enter accelerate; repeat to skip.

Controller: D-pad/left stick navigate, A/× confirm, B/○ back, X/□ card details, hold Y/△ to pass. During replay A accelerates, repeat A skips; B skips when no modal is open. Start opens/closes the battle menu. LB/RB switch tabs, pages or placement rows. L3/Up from hand inspects the board; B returns to hand. R3 activates the leader. Hold LT for your graveyard, RT for deck, both for opponent graveyard. Right stick scrolls descriptions. System accept/back swapping is supported.

Four menu tabs: Actions, Effects, Audio and game, Controls. A changes settings and B closes the menu during replay. Disabled items remain focusable with a reason; changing values retains focus. Battle hints appear in the right sidebar.

Animated/static leaders are saved with the game. Tempo and brightness are independent. History retains 64 actions and 128 events per action, with source/target and numeric changes.
