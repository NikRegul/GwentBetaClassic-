# Gwent Beta Classic

**You must back up your saves before installing the mod and before every update. Bugs can still occur; use a separate test save for your first matches.**

Gwent Beta 0.9.24 cards and rules inside The Witcher 3.

Replaces the game's built-in Gwent with five factions, three rows, bronze,
silver and gold cards, leaders, weather, Spies, Ambushes and ability combinations.

## Features

- 479 collectible cards, including 21 leaders, across Monsters, Northern Realms,
  Nilfgaard, Scoia'tael and Skellige.
- Deck builder with eight saved slots, renaming, search and filters. Select your
  deck before an NPC match, or edit it separately from the game's deck menu.
- Five starter decks and a full catalog, including unowned cards and their
  descriptions, tags and acquisition sources.
- Random prepared decks for ordinary opponents and fixed decks for quest players.
- One eligible bronze/silver card for each of the first four wins against each
  ordinary NPC. Quest players award an assigned gold card, or 300 crowns if owned.
- 150-crown kegs at the Crow's Perch quartermaster. Buy several and open them
  individually from inventory or the deck menu: up to four bronze cards and
  one rare choice from three options, without excess copies.
- Collection and custom decks stored in your game save; vanilla merchant cards
  replaced with comparable Beta cards.
- Mulligans, automatic rounds, precise unit insertion, direct special targeting,
  both graveyards and your own deck view. Deck viewing does not reveal draw order.
- Full card inspection with base power, text and tags; opponent's deck stays hidden.
- Original artwork, sound effects and battle voices from the Beta.
- Mouse, keyboard and controller support with contextual hints.
- Shared AI logic and 40 adapted archetype profiles.

## Languages and installation

Choose one full RU or EN package; each contains its own text and voices.
Close the game, back up your save, and extract Mods into The Witcher 3 folder.
You should have Mods/modBetaGwent0924/content. Replace the previous mod folder
when updating; do not install both variants. REDkit and Wwise are not needed.
Target: PC game 5.0; earlier-version compatibility has not been established.
Other Gwent menu, reward and card-trade mods may conflict.

Open the game's Gwent deck menu to edit a deck, or talk to an NPC to play.
Collection and decks survive language-package changes; custom names stay unchanged.

## Controller

D-pad/left stick navigates; A/× confirms; B/○ backs out; X/□ inspects;
hold Y/△ to pass. LB/RB (L1/R1) selects rows during placement or pages in lists.
View/Touchpad opens your deck, LT/RT (L2/R2) opens graveyards, L3 inspects
the board, R3 uses the leader, and Start/Options opens actions. Deck building,
renaming and saving are supported without a mouse. Full controls are in the ZIP.

## Current state

This is the first public version. Rules target historical Beta 0.9.24, while
the board and animations still differ from the original. Closer presentation
and AI tuning from real matches are next. Broader quest/tournament, old-save/NG+
and compatibility coverage remains. Every possible card interaction has not
been exhaustively verified. Concrete deck and game-state reports are welcome.
