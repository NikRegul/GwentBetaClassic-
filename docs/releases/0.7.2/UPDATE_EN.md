# Gwent Beta Classic 0.7.2

Beta additive materials now render as light rather than opaque paint. Blood, smoke, poison drops, bubbles and AlphaBlended banish particles retain their original blending.

Card power effects use individual impacts with bounded size and softer boosts. Physical shots use the Beta crescent and growing trail without the opaque tip mesh. Clear Skies/First Light gain warm diagonal full-screen rays recreated from the reference footage. Fire destruction, Igni, Epidemic, Yrden and placement/summon highlights use their source material blending. The accepted lightning is preserved.

Rows compact after destruction finishes. The effects gallery starts separately from battle controls and keeps its exit/navigation available on effect errors. Includes the editor, keg and logic audit improvements from 0.7.1.

Each package includes 13 text languages. GUI target: 65 MiB. Automated build checks do not replace an in-game visual check; this release's new visuals have not yet been tested in game.
