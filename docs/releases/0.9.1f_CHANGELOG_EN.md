# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.1f

October 21, 2026. AI update over 0.9.1e for Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+.

## Preserve cards after an opposing pass

Fixed useful low-body ability cards being missed by bounded search. After an opposing pass, one existing exact-simulation slot is assigned to a cheap compound ability, such as playing from a deck or replaying a card. Simulation counts and search depth stay unchanged.

For example, the AI could spend Mourntart while overlooking a two-power Slyzard. In a reproduced position Slyzard actually catches up in round two, preserving Mourntart to win the final. This position came from randomized self-play investigating the hand-preservation patterns observed in the user's games.

The AI evaluates the real ability resolution; it does not have to choose a cheap card that fails to win the required round. Regression checks cover both synchronous and frame-distributed search and independence from hidden opposing hand identities.

## Redundant reactive copies

After an opposing dry pass, the AI no longer reserves every identical Bronze damage card for future targets. It can win the empty round with one copy, preserving the other and its gold finisher. A confirmed example plays one of two Ekhidnas instead of Imlerith: Sabbath, changing the final from a loss to a win. The previous reserve remains when the opponent is active or only one copy remains.

## Validation and randomized games

Eight completed human games were examined from the available log. Recorded actions, passes and displayed scores are extracted without inventing unlogged cards. This is not claimed to be a bit-for-bit reproduction of private deals.

Automatic tests randomize opponents, deals and turn order. The candidate and previous policy share initial states and play both decks in both seats. A separate random holdout is used after selecting the change. The diagnostic runner no longer calls a tied round a cheap win when that tie immediately loses the match.

## Included from 0.9.1e

- Round economy, maximum-difficulty starting-list estimates for Scorch/Igni, Schirru and Toruviel pass risk.
- Direct Panther board targeting, retaining the limit of at most three units in the target row.
- Henry var Attre can finish with zero selections. Mangonel responds to reveals in either hand during its owner's turn.
- Original Beta main-menu music in the deck builder. Install the included `modBetaGwentMenuMusic` together with the main module.

No starting five-point bonus has been added. Automated checks and native compilation do not replace in-game UI and audio acceptance testing.

The final code is checked with the full regression suite and a fresh randomized schedule after selecting both fixes.

The final code passed 48 regression suites, 1,426 tactical scenarios and 5,632 additional positions. Saved positions provide 41 checks of both search modes, hidden-information independence and card preservation. A fresh independent schedule completed 368 randomized paired games against 0.9.1e: 181 wins, 183 losses and 4 draws, with no errors. This sample does not confirm an overall strength increase. Two specifically repaired losing continuations were verified separately and are not presented as an overall win-rate measurement.
