# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.1g

On every difficulty, the AI no longer rewards an expensive play for extra immediate points when they require no additional opposing card. It preserves stronger cards and its leader in those positions while retaining a small score margin. Engine development, timers and future ability utility are protected from that discount. A complete fixed continuation confirms a saved finisher changing a 32:34 loss into a 36:34 win.

The AI evaluates catch-up sequences more accurately after the opponent passes. When its one- and two-action search finds no winning line, it resolves a bounded selection of three-action continuations on the changing board. This can discover graveyard boosts followed by resurrection instead of merely adding independent card estimates. All difficulties retain the round resource budget; preparation runs in small steps.

Fixed a first-round pass shortcut: equal hand sizes and a 26-point lead no longer bypass the already calculated opposing response envelope. High difficulty uses the announced starting list without reading the player's current hidden hand. A safe large lead still permits passing to preserve cards, including when one card behind.

Validation includes 49 regression suites, with 155 checks for the new search and passing behaviour, plus native Classic 1.32 and NextGen compilation. Corrected tactical examples do not establish an overall increase in win rate against humans. Version comparisons and deck rankings are reported separately after their full experiments finish.

All 0.9.1f changes remain, including arrow targeting for Panther with its row limit, optional Henry selection, ambush-aware passing, more economical catch-up choices and separate main-menu music in the deck builder. No starting five-point bonus was added. Deck compositions and card rules were not changed in 0.9.1g.

Native gameplay and music playback in the installed game have not been verified for this build. Building it did not modify the installed game or save files.
