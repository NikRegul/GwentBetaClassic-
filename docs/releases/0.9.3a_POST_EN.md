# Update 0.9.3a

Ordinary AI now accounts for winning passes, an exhausted opponent's remaining actions and an available drawing fallback in short endgames. It still seeks a winning continuation before accepting a draw.

Retains all 0.9.3 features, including Classic difficulty and the selected original profiles. Ordinary AI has not gained access to the opponent's hidden hand. Technical checks and separate full games do not replace a strength trial. The running tournament keeps its previous frozen version.

Separate Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+ packages. No publication or automatic installation.
