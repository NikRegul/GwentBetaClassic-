# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.1h

The AI now considers the combinations remaining in its hand after a move. Consuming the last persistent setup for remaining payoff cards adds a bounded resource cost. A held replacement reduces this cost. One-shot preparation, such as Draig's boost, is not treated as a persistent dependency.

For a limited set of promising moves, the AI resolves actual abilities on a copied position, checks three permutations of its remaining deck and models a bounded opponent response. It uses public cards and information permitted by difficulty, including the initial deck list at maximum difficulty. It does not receive the player's hidden hand. Possible Scorch exposure contributes a bounded risk estimate rather than a proof of defeat.

This reserve applies at every difficulty in rounds where resources can be saved. It does not hold cards back in the final round or when the opponent already has a crown. Work is split into small steps for the game interface.

This is a limited improvement to move evaluation, not a complete multi-round search tree. Deck compositions, starting points, accepted effects, interface and music carry over from g.

The update includes 70 focused checks and 51 regression suites. Final build and short comparison results are stored alongside the archives. A short benchmark cannot guarantee a statistically established improvement over g. Both Classic 1.32 and NextGen 5.0+ compilation are checked; installed native gameplay has not been manually verified.
