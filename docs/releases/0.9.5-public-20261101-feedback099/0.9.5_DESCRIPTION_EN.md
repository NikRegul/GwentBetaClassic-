# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.5

## Changes in 0.9.5

- Deck editor: removed the leader note and extra gap before the cards. Counter backgrounds and numbers align with the list. The preview, including its banner, fits the right recess. Removed the repeated generic opponent label while keeping actual names.

- Hard: removed the fixed 26-point minimum for passing. In a developed optional round, the decision weighs public reply tempo, card disadvantage and the cost of another play, preserving the last cards instead of buying more score margin.

- The AI forecasts the next opening board through actual cleanup: resilience, Morkvarg returning, weakening and locking. When a dry pass would leave it behind, it can preserve its hand and concede initiative; sufficient resources still allow pressure. Hard does not read hidden cards or future draws.

- 0.9.5 rebuild: corrected Olaf’s Beast history, Xavier Moran’s template-strength bonus, Ciri: Dash’s Resilience and Morkvarg’s weakening in the graveyard before returning. Restored the preview card size, added counter-panel insets and the original editor effect for reaching 25 cards.

- Further beta rules checks: Cursed Knight fully resets another copy of itself; Hubert Rejk drains only boosted deck cards and does not heal damaged ones. Zero current and base strength in an inactive zone causes banishment without Deathwish; Morkvarg weakened to zero is banished in the graveyard.

- Olgierd returns at turn end only after being killed: discarding him from hand does not enable his return, and leaving the graveyard clears the killed flag.

- Primary branches of all 511 cards now have independent checks; every ability combination is not yet proven. Fixed optional selections, shuffles and card order, Francesca, Lugos, Agitator, False Ciri and Wolfsbane. The nine deferred items are addressed: Bloody Flail spawns its token even without a target; False Ciri preserves her row index; Emhyr returns a card at turn end; Monster Nest boosts its child before deploy; Dimun’s random stream and Ge’els’ return order are corrected, and Yennefer’s empty pause is removed. Definition-pool order is verified. All five modes of both Shupe forms are checked in every offered slot; the Hunter’s 15-damage mode can target either side.

- New beta-style deck and collection menus: faction deck-name header, six-column grid, square filters and search, and a crafting panel on the right. Added deck-row and copy-counter animations, controller button hints and pass-hold feedback on the coin.

- Updated card choice and replacement windows, results with card/scrap rewards, history, match menu, deck/graveyard viewers and import/confirmation dialogs. Separate keyword explanations, aligned controller hints and window sounds are included. This is a functional adaptation; exact artwork, original animations and in-game acceptance remain incomplete.

- Cancel directed targeting with right-click, Escape or gamepad Back before the ability performs its first action. Arachas Queen returns to the leader slot before the first consume. A nested unit returns to placement without undoing its parent card. Deck/graveyard card offers cannot be cancelled. Fixed atlas frames and continuous scrolling in mulligan, deck and graveyard windows; reward art and progress use the received card’s faction, including Neutral. AI weather forecasts spend armour once and account for a weaker neighbour dying.

- Fixed coloured crown artifacts, parchment seams and button centering when switching mouse/controller. Removed the description wheel hint and restored the armour-break sound. Emissary must play an available card. Directed plays remain tentative until the first action: Roach and other reactions wait for target confirmation. Harald evaluates full row damage and an opposing pass followed by Frost; with an empty hand he preserves the leader when an optional round is still lost.

The 0.9.4/0.9.4a save format is unchanged. No overall strength increase is claimed. Automated acceptance does not replace in-game checks on Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+.

**More decks. Smarter turns. Gwent from the beta era.**

Bring open-beta Gwent into The Witcher 3: familiar cards, a full deck editor and matches against characters across the world. Public 0.9.3 brings together the changes since 0.9.1 and fixes from recent play logs.

## Changes in 0.9.4a

- Deck editor: adding and removing a card now plays the Beta effects UIDBCardAdd and UIDBCardRemove, ported from the Beta files. The effect appears on the collection card and is not shown with reduced effects.

- Leader and faction are chosen only when a deck is created. Previously the faction buttons in the editor could replace the leader and remove cards from the deck. Importing into an open deck accepts only a code with its leader and faction.

- Editor filters and search show results from the first card, and late game replies no longer disturb the editor.

- Gamepad: the aiming arrow is shown again when choosing a target, as with the mouse.

- Spectral Whale now always moves to a different row at turn end, as in the Beta, and never stays in its own. If no other row has room, it stays and still deals its damage.

- AI: Hard places Sheldon Skaggs on a row where he really scatters units and gains his boost. Classic no longer passes because of an unfinished calculation when it has a good move.

- AI accounts for ships' end-of-turn effects after the opponent passes and preserves the selected row when placing a card at its end.

- Deck counters highlight gold and silver limit violations and both bounds of the 25–40 card limit.

Saves from 0.9.4 work as they are: the store format did not change. Automated checks and both compilers do not replace an in-game test.

## Changes in 0.9.4

- Next-Gen 5.x saves only the first 45 mod-added fields of Geralt, and that limit is shared by every installed mod. Game facts keep only 16-bit numbers. Beta Gwent used more fields, so with other mods installed, decks, kegs and scraps could vanish after loading. The whole profile now lives in a single four-field store, and the facts backup is written in parts that fit 16 bits.

- Recovery is also hardened: the profile loads before its first change; the damaged original and quarantined decks are preserved for recovery, and an unknown future format is read-only. Format 2 reads format 1 and validates text length and integrity together with the numeric data before accepting a record. Data absent from both the save and available backups cannot be restored.

- Existing saves are migrated automatically on first load.

- After the tournament, an AI decision involving a spy in a short round was corrected. Development sample: 368 games, 50%, zero errors. The holdout was not run; an overall strength gain has not been established. Hard still observes player-visible information limits; Classic has broader access to hidden information.

For Next-Gen 5.0+ and Classic 1.32. On 1.32 the old fields are still saved next to the new store. Going back to 0.9.3 after saving with 0.9.4 is not supported. Automated checks and native compilation do not replace an in-game save, quit and load test.

## Features

- 30 approved original beta decks join our 46:76 ordinary lineups and 33 thematic quest-opponent presets,92 unique compositions. Imported deck names are retained.15 similar decks await review;28 invalid deck constructions are excluded.

- Classic is a separate fourth difficulty using the ported beta AI.24 original profiles serve 109 deck presets. Choose it before a match beside the language controls. Classic sees your hand and deck; ordinary Hard retains its information restrictions.

- Hard better evaluates follow-ups after leaders, card order in short decisive rounds, drawing before conceding and useful passes. Boosts account for Menno and power resets; damage accounts for Clan an Craite Greatsword recovery. Reveal decks weigh opposing hidden cards against useful own-hand synergies. With a hidden ambush, the reply tree now considers a bounded set of publicly possible traps without reading the concealed card. Two-trap worlds correctly cover timer order and copies drawn without replacement. One simulated action per step is retained; this fix does not establish an overall strength gain.

- Offered cards now have their own choice window: Cantarella clearly shows what to keep and what returns to the deck bottom. Hidden cards stay face down. Opponent names replace internal profiles; the redundant Your Deck heading and unused space below the leader are removed. Controller White Frost focus, difficulty visibility, empty-hand auto-pass and wasted weather after an opposing pass are fixed. Card-choice windows now explain the outcome: with Udalryk the chosen card goes to your hand and the other to your graveyard. Other choices where it matters where the chosen and remaining cards go are captioned the same way; the rest show the card’s ability text.

- 13 text languages with independent voices in Russian, English, German, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese. Fixed voice selection leaving dialogue in Russian. Auto follows the game speech language.

Additional fixes: Cyclops accounts for the consumed unit’s damage and full An Craite Swordsman recovery when enough turns remain. Kayran consuming Miruna and poison/Elven Saber target dispatch are corrected. Classic 1.32 RNG avoids the faulty remainder operation. Damaged custom slots recover from validated backup banks while surviving decks and original damaged data are preserved. Unknown schemas cannot overwrite the backup. In-game save/load acceptance is still required. The same stored-damage correction also covers Madman Lugos.

## Compatibility and validation

For Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+. Accepted artwork, VFX and music are preserved. Automated checks and native compilation do not replace in-game UI, voice and RNG acceptance. Historical strength trials apply to their frozen versions; universal superiority of this build is not claimed.
