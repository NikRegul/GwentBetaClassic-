# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.1c — AI and spawned-card fixes

20 October 2026. Hotfix on top of 0.9.1: AI, spawned-card pools and tooltips are corrected; decks, audio and art are preserved. Applies to both Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+ packages. Includes all 0.9.1a and 0.9.1b fixes. Full 0.9.1 changelog: [0.9.1_CHANGELOG_EN.md](0.9.1_CHANGELOG_EN.md).

## New in 0.9.1c

- Miruna brings the spy back even when the opponent has an empty hand or has passed: the charm fires at the start of the next AI turn. Previously the return was ignored there, and in round 3 at 13:18 AI passed holding Double Cross, although Double Cross pulling the spy under Miruna won the round.
- The return is counted only when Miruna's timer can reach zero within the AI turns left.
- A last deck-pulling card (Double Cross and similar) is played even with a useless target when passing would concede the match.

## Spawned cards and tooltips

- Dimeritium Shackles can target a face-down enemy Ambush. The UI no longer treats its concealed rarity as an invalid tier; identity, tier, power and countdown remain hidden until revealed. Ordinary damage restrictions, immunity and the special-cancelling Ambush reaction are preserved.
- Vanghemar now spawns Biting Frost, Clear Skies or Shrike, matching Beta 0.9.24. Incorrect Impenetrable Fog and Arachas Venom options are removed.
- Ida Emean now spawns Impenetrable Fog, Clear Skies or Alzur’s Thunder, replacing the incorrect Torrential Rain option.
- Their full card views show all three related cards and open the actual rules. Clear Skies remains an internal option, outside the collectible catalogue. Russian title declension no longer hides Shrike.
- The Russian Spawn imperative now includes the original Spawn glossary definition. Existing Beta definitions provide its translation.
- All eight menus are recompiled for Classic and Next-Gen, preserving accepted artwork and platform adaptations.

## From 0.9.1b

- AI no longer passes its last card in a deciding round when the pass concedes the match. Previously it passed at 12:20 in round 3 holding the spy, whose draw could have found Mourntart.
- While a Nekker is on the board and Nekker Warrior is in hand, own consumers (Forktail, Vran Warrior, Kayran, Barbegazi) wait: Nekker Warrior first adds two Nekker copies to the deck, so every consumed Deathwish summons one.
- The between-round mulligan no longer returns held finishers (Mourntart, Scorch and similar) to the deck.
- Double Cross accounts for the spy it pulls from the deck being placed opposite a waiting Miruna and charmed back; it was previously rejected as useless.
- Slyzard without a real target (a bronze grave unit with a copy in the deck) is not played for nothing. In a lost optional round where every hand card is needed later, AI passes and keeps its hand instead of dumping cards.
- The spy gets Miruna ordering priority only when it is the unit she will charm, not a stronger enemy unit already opposite her.

## From 0.9.1a

- Miruna is not played against an empty enemy board, except for the Miruna -> spy combo; with a target, she goes opposite it.
- The spy forecast counts Miruna's return, so AI plays the spy while Miruna waits opposite an empty row.
- In a deciding round, Vran Warrior and other consumers eat their own board Nekker when a deck copy remains.

All cases were reproduced from gameplay logs and are covered by regressions (check_miruna_timing091, check_nekker_food091, check_playtest091b, check_playtest091c) at several difficulty levels.
