# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.1i

The AI now checks a separate continuation after each modeled opponent reply: our card → a plausible reply → our next card. Fully revealed hands use the worst examined reply; unknown hands use bounded public hypotheses rather than the actual hidden cards.

Easy and normal difficulty now also resolve promising card plays through the real rules and examine limited continuations. Search breadth and depth vary by difficulty. Only maximum difficulty receives the initial enemy deck list; hidden hands and future draw order remain private.

A bounded complete search handles small public final rounds with plain units and Scorch: at most six available cards, with no active leaders, weather or unsupported abilities. Both players can pass. A budget cutoff is unknown, not a proof of victory or defeat. An already winning pass takes priority over spending another card.

Search work is split into callbacks with at most one simulated card play each. The resource-saving rules from h remain. Decks, starting points, visuals and music are unchanged. The deck editor uses Beta's main menu theme; matches retain the existing Witcher 3 music.

New checks compare public finals with an independent arithmetic solver and verify distinct reply continuations, budgets, information boundaries, live state/RNG preservation and synchronous/cooperative agreement. Final benchmark and compiler results are stored next to the archives. Headless checks and successful compilation do not replace manual native gameplay verification.

This improves tactical planning. Full multi-round search, joint hidden hand/deck hypotheses and exhaustive ability target enumeration remain future work. Short comparisons against h are not a new complete deck ranking.
