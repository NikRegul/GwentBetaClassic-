# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.1e

October 21, 2026. Update for Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+. Includes the fixes from 0.9.1d and earlier releases.

## AI

- The AI can pass with a large safe lead even when behind on cards. Points gifted by a draw spy no longer require it to keep spending its hand.
- In round one, the AI estimates the card commitment needed to overtake the opponent. It can concede an optional round when chasing would leave it two cards behind. A possible one-card catch and rounds that must be won retain priority.
- In optional rounds, excess immediate points have less value when they do not force another opposing card. Expensive cards can be preserved for later rounds.
- Round-two pressure and preserving a long final now depend on the remaining hand, including engines, timers and independent bodies. The plan is reconsidered as the hand changes.
- At maximum difficulty, early-pass estimates of Scorch and Igni use the known starting list and subtract publicly played copies. Schirru remains a possible source of Scorch. The AI does not inspect the current hidden hand, deck order or private draws.
- A concealed opposing ambush is considered a possible Toruviel, including nearby boosts, when passing. Its hidden identity is not read.
- Includes the late deciding-round card-order fixes.

No starting five-point bonus has been added.

## Cards

- Panther selects its target directly on the board with an arrow. The target row must still contain fewer than four units: at most three.
- Henry var Attre can finish selection after concealing zero, some or all eligible cards.
- Mangonel reacts to reveals in either hand during its owner's turn. Reveals during the opponent's turn and concealment do not trigger a shot.

## Deck-builder music

The original Gwent main-menu theme from the Beta assets now loops in the deck builder. It uses the music bus independently of voices and combat effects. Leaving the editor stops the theme and resumes regular music. Install the separate `modBetaGwentMenuMusic` module together with the main mod.

## Validation

Logic checks use frozen sources. Both platform packages require native compilation and archive content, CRC and SHA-256 verification. Scenario regressions reproduce specific mistakes; they do not establish an overall win-rate increase. Automated matches and compilation do not replace in-game UI and audio acceptance testing.

The final code passed 47 regression suites, 1,426 tactical scenarios and 5,632 additional positions. The expanded round-economy suite contains 277 checks. In 368 paired games against the previous version it scored 180 wins, 180 losses and 8 draws, with no errors. This comparison does not confirm an overall strength increase.
