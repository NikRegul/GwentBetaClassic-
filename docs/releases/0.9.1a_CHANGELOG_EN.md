# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.1a — AI fixes

20 October 2026. Hotfix on top of 0.9.1: AI scripts only; UI, decks, audio and art are unchanged. Applies to both Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+ packages. Full 0.9.1 changelog: [0.9.1_CHANGELOG_EN.md](0.9.1_CHANGELOG_EN.md).

## AI — Arachas Swarm

- Miruna is no longer played against an empty enemy board with nothing to charm. The exception is the spy combo: Miruna first, the spy into her opposite row next turn, and her charm brings the spy back.
- With a target on the board, Miruna goes to the row opposite it, keeping a free slot for the charmed unit.
- While Miruna waits opposite an empty row, AI plays the spy instead of another card such as Dandelion. The spy forecast now counts Miruna returning it.
- In a deciding round, Vran Warrior and other consumers eat their own board Nekker when a deck copy remains, since its Deathwish summons that copy. Nekkers are still held as finishers in earlier rounds. Reproduced from a gameplay log where a Vran Warrior ate a 3-power unit instead of the Nekker and lost the round.

Covered by regressions (check_miruna_timing091, check_nekker_food091) at several difficulty levels.
