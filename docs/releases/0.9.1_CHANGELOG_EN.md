# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.1 — changelog

20 October 2026. Changes from 0.9.0 final17. Separate Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+ packages. Both are 0.9.1 candidates pending gameplay checks.

## Both platforms

- Restored Rally's original illustration and normal card frame in First Light's choice screen, replacing the question-mark placeholder. The random bronze unit is still selected only after confirming Rally.
- Fixed the targeting arrow for created spells: Alzur's Thunder from Vaedermakar points from its caster, including Vaedermakar played through Marching Orders. A previous selection or completed action no longer replaces the current ability's source. Leader abilities use their own seat.
- Fixed wasting Brewess: Ritual in an empty second round after the opponent passes. When a cheaper card wins the round, AI preserves Brewess, Nekkers and a Slyzard with a Nekker follow-up for the deciding round. It still uses them when needed to catch up and save the match. Reproduced from an Eggs/Consume gameplay log and checked across difficulty levels and simulation budgets.
- Fixed the gamepad hand preview freezing on a different card from the selected card. Roll events from the stationary mouse cursor no longer override controller focus while hand cards move. The same protection applies to mulligan and graveyard previews. Moving the mouse returns mouse control.
- D-pad left/right can continue beyond the edge of the hand or board row to an available deck/graveyard pile. Hidden-card inspection restrictions, LT/RT pile shortcuts and the Y action menu remain.
- Rebuilt all four menu entry points separately for each platform, preserving its native textures and image definitions. Classic keeps its subimage rendering and TestMenu keg registration. Five mirrored AS source copies retain UTF-8 BOM and CRLF.
- AI now plans hand conservation, second-round pressure and baiting expensive answers, adapting to public actions. Cheap round wins and saving the match take priority. Card choices account for combo reserves, future threats and retaining last-play control.
- On high difficulty, AI knows the starting deck list declared when the match begins. It does not read the current hand, deck order or actual remaining deck. Only publicly revealed copies are removed from the unknown pool. Easy/medium do not use the exact list; raising difficulty mid-match does not recover it from hidden cards.
- Improved Arachas Swarm timing and consumption: preserve Nekkers and Mourntart as finishers, prepare Miruna for her timer, avoid consuming valuable Nekkers/charged Behemoths, prefer Olgierd as food, and coordinate Miruna, the spy and Double Cross. Prepare Mourntart and Stregobor with their supporting cards.
- Adda mulligan now accounts for spell tutors and Bloodsucker synergy. Sabrina finishing is checked by simulation across all suitable damage cards, duels, tutors and orders.
- Ambush evaluation includes the effects of Morenn, Morenn: Forest Child, Malena and Toruviel instead of printed power alone. Impera Infantry gains preparation value from allied spies in hand. Consuming an Archespore accounts for its four-damage Deathwish and follow-up consumers.

- 56, Arachas Swarm: replaced with the author's complete Queen-led swarm/consume list using Miruna, Dandelion: Poet, Triss: Sorceress, Kayran, Olgierd, Double Cross, the spy, Nest, Stregobor, Mourntart and five bronze playsets.
- 58, Adda: two Kaedweni Cavalry replaced by two Kaedweni Revenants; Reinforcement replaced by Sabrina's Specter. Sabrina stays.

- 74, Deathwish: rebuilt around Archespores and Deathwish/consume synergies.
- 95, Usurper: Assire replaced by Guardian.
- 94, Aretuza: John Natalis replaced by Philippa Eilhart; adepts supply Frost and spell tutors supply Thunder.
- 93, Dryads: three Mahakam Guards replaced by three Vrihedd Sappers. All four Beta dryads stay.
- 96, Bran wounds/swordsmen: Triss: Telekinesis replaced by Wild Boar of the Sea.
- 67, Nekkers: roster unchanged; ineffective experimental rules reverted.

The complete before/after ranking of all 46 decks is unfinished. Preliminary AI-vs-AI win rates are not a human tier list or a confirmed final improvement.

## Classic 1.32 only

- Restored `bgboard_open()` and `bgboard_open(preset)` practice entry through a working stock menu. Opponent selection and practice reward restrictions remain; ordinary and quest matches keep their native entry path.
- Fixed the known cause of the infinite loading screen at the Seven Cats notice board before the Hearts of Stone q601 scene: the Gwent sound bank is no longer permanently loaded.
- Restored the exact vanilla 1.32 always-load list, containing 303 banks. Removed 18 Next-Gen-only bank entries as well.
- The Gwent bank remains available and is requested by the mod interface, then released on close. Voices and effects were confirmed audible in a match; all five language bank loads and bank release on close were confirmed natively.
- Fixed the failure to load the 249 MiB five-language bank on demand. Classic has a 400 MiB total sound-bank budget. Language-specific embedded banks now use 76.4 MiB for Russian and 116.5–121.8 MiB for other selections, retaining the encoded audio bytes and original events. Only the selected bank is loaded; changing voice language releases the previous bank, and closing the menu releases the active bank. None is permanently loaded.
- The global list fix removes the same precondition for other sound-streaming scenes; no other specific quest hang has been confirmed.

## Build and validation

- Both packages identify as 0.9.1. The internal `091b` deck-data name is not a separate public release.
- Generated AI roster/strategy tables are frozen with AI logic and included in both native compilations. Regression inputs must match the compiled sources.
- Preserved accepted final17 effects, lightning, weather, card dissolves, art, 13 text languages, five voice languages, clipboard support, profile/shop fixes, Classic rewards and keg fixes.
- Immutable candidate sources, regression suites, both native compiler results and archive CRC/file sizes/SHA-256 are verified before delivery.
- Outdated Script Merger output overriding Beta Gwent scripts must be regenerated after updating.
- Gameplay acceptance remains: controller previews and piles, mulligan, match, deck editor, keg opening, audio/voices and save/load on both platforms; q601 both after a fresh launch and after a completed match on 1.32.
