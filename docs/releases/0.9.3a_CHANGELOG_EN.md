# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.3a

AI decision fixes over 0.9.3 for Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+.

- Search retains a winning answering pass instead of forcing another hand card.
- After the opponent's last card, resolves the possible match ending, including leader availability and reactions to both passes. A reproduced position now preserves its strong unit instead of choosing a self-destructive Scorch.
- Keeps an available draw as a fallback while still searching for a winning continuation.
- Short lines use complete-position scores rather than the legacy per-card uplift.
- Replacing an accepted leader catch-up with a hand card checks the actual card budget again. Permission to play a leader without spending a hand card no longer silently authorizes an over-budget card investment in an optional round.
- Preparation stays cooperative, with at most one simulated card or pass action per callback. Ordinary AI retains its existing hidden-information restrictions.

Includes the unchanged Classic difficulty with 24 original profiles and 109 preset bindings, 30 approved original Beta decks, thematic NPC decks, pre-match difficulty selection, empty-hand auto-pass, White Frost controller focus, and the accepted UI, VFX, covers and music. Original Classic AI logic is unchanged; that difficulty can inspect the opponent's actual hand and deck.

The candidate passed regressions and separate full technical games. These checks do not establish a general strength gain. The running knockout continues on frozen 0.9.3 inputs and is not a test of 0.9.3a. Native gameplay acceptance of RNG/interface behavior remains a separate step. No automatic installation.
