# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.5

- Deck editor: removed the leader note and extra gap before the cards. Counter backgrounds and numbers align with the list. The preview, including its banner, fits the right recess. Removed the repeated generic opponent label while keeping actual names.

- Hard: removed the fixed 26-point minimum for passing. In a developed optional round, the decision weighs public reply tempo, card disadvantage and the cost of another play, preserving the last cards instead of buying more score margin.

- The AI forecasts the next opening board through actual cleanup: resilience, Morkvarg returning, weakening and locking. When a dry pass would leave it behind, it can preserve its hand and concede initiative; sufficient resources still allow pressure. Hard does not read hidden cards or future draws.

- 0.9.5 rebuild: corrected Olaf’s Beast history, Xavier Moran’s template-strength bonus, Ciri: Dash’s Resilience and Morkvarg’s weakening in the graveyard before returning. Restored the preview card size, added counter-panel insets and the original editor effect for reaching 25 cards.

- Further beta rules checks: Cursed Knight fully resets another copy of itself; Hubert Rejk drains only boosted deck cards and does not heal damaged ones. Zero current and base strength in an inactive zone causes banishment without Deathwish; Morkvarg weakened to zero is banished in the graveyard.

- Olgierd returns at turn end only after being killed: discarding him from hand does not enable his return, and leaving the graveyard clears the killed flag.

- New beta-style deck and collection menus: faction deck-name header, six-column grid, square filters and search, and a crafting panel on the right. Added deck-row and copy-counter animations, controller button hints and pass-hold feedback on the coin.

- Updated card choice and replacement windows, results with card/scrap rewards, history, match menu, deck/graveyard viewers and import/confirmation dialogs. Separate keyword explanations, aligned controller hints and window sounds are included. This is a functional adaptation; exact artwork, original animations and in-game acceptance remain incomplete.

The 0.9.4/0.9.4a save format is unchanged. No overall strength increase is claimed. Automated acceptance does not replace in-game checks on Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+.
