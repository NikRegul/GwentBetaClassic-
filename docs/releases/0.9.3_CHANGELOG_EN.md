# Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.3

Update for Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+. Includes 0.9.2 changes and a separate fourth difficulty, Classic.

## Classic

- Uses the ported original Beta AI evaluation, AlphaBeta search, strategic adjustments, card/target/row selection and mulligans. The 109 ready deck and quest variants are assigned 24 original named profiles, representing 17 distinct effective settings, using compositions, leaders and authored Beta bindings. The five faction Practice profiles also remain available for decks without a ready assignment.
- The opponent sees the player's actual hand and remaining deck. The difficulty tooltip explains this. Deck construction and action legality remain shared.
- Select Classic before a match. Switching into or out of it applies to the next match. Easy, Medium and Hard retain their existing policies and information limits.
- Calculation runs in small steps on the shared battle engine. Playing requires no JavaScript, Node.js or .NET runtime.

## Decks

- Adds 30 distinct original Beta decks under their source file names. The ordinary opponent pool grows from 46 to 76, including migration of an existing saved rotation.
- Fifteen similar decks and duplicates remain held for the user's decision. Twenty-eight construction-invalid source decks are excluded.
- Thirty-three quest opponents receive thematic compositions based on researched archetypes. Quest bindings and rewards are preserved. Composition review does not establish measured strength against human decks.

## Shared rules and randomness

- Corrected Daerlan and Fire Scorpion reveal ownership, random placement during card moves, and Cow/Trapper child cards.
- Corrected Shield/Spear selection stages, Jotunn's movement cap based on free slots, Saskia's whole-set replacement and Black Blood's target categories.
- Random generation no longer relies on integer overflow and checks the source reference vectors. If the game runtime fails that contract, a separately marked fallback captures 1024 game-engine values. Its finite cycle is copied with the generator during simulation.
- Repeated Classic 1.32 deals were confirmed in logs despite different seeds. The proposed fix has automated and compiler checks; acceptance in the installed game remains pending.

Accepted artwork, battle effects, music and controller fixes are preserved. No automatic installation. Tournament results and technical evidence are separate; source-port parity alone does not establish playing strength.

Profile selection completed 688 screening and 1344 separate confirmation games without game errors. Candidates had higher confirmation point estimates for 32 compositions; this finite sample does not establish globally strongest profiles or a general advantage over Hard. The historical 1104-game tournament covered only five Practice profiles and does not describe these new assignments. Not all 229 historical profiles or unsupported search backends are claimed as implemented modes.

## New playtest fixes

- Difficulty is directly available beside the language controls in match setup. The original Beta option is named Classic, with its hidden-information access still explained.
- White Frost moves controller focus directly to legal rows.
- Empty-hand automatic pass resumes after replay when the leader has been spent; unresolved choices and an available leader remain under player control.
- The emergency anti-concession guard no longer reselects rejected, ineffective weather after the opponent passes. Plays with actual gain or a useful own timer remain eligible.
