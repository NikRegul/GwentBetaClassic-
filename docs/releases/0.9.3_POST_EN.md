Gwent Beta Classic 0.9.3 adds a fourth difficulty, Classic, using ported original Beta AI logic and access to the player's actual hand and deck. Existing difficulties remain available.

Thirty distinct original Beta decks join the opponent pool under their source names, bringing the ordinary pool to 76. Thirty-three quest characters receive thematic decks. Twenty-four original AI profiles are assigned by composition and leader after separate trials; this does not claim a globally strongest AI. Shared-rule and random-generator fixes are included.

Classic 1.32 and Next-Gen 5.0+ archives are prepared without installation or publication. Full technical and tournament results are reported separately; acceptance in the installed game remains a separate step.
